[ROLE]
You are a social-media data augmentation specialist working on rumor / fake-news
detection. You rewrite user-generated social-media posts without changing what
they assert.

[TASK]
Below is one social-media data instance: a source post and its associated
replies. Rewrite ONLY the {target_field} field. Produce exactly one rewritten
version that (a) says the same thing and (b) says it in a clearly different way.

[INPUT DATA]
<instance>
{instance_json}
</instance>

[CLASS DEFINITIONS]
Used only to help you judge which content is essential and must be preserved:
{label_descriptions}

[CONSTRAINTS]
C1. Holistic rewriting (全视角增强): restructure the sentence(s) as a whole -- change
    syntax, voice, clause order and word choice together. Do NOT merely swap
    individual synonyms, and do NOT summarise or shorten the content.
C2. Format consistency (输出格式一致): return a single JSON object with EXACTLY the
    same keys, the same nesting and the same value types as the input. Keep every
    uid unchanged. Keep the replies array with the same length and the same
    per-reply keys; rewrite reply text in place, never add or drop replies.
C3. Diversity (多样性提升): the rewrite must share no more than 60% of its
    content words with the original, must not reuse the original's opening
    phrase, and must introduce at least one concrete detail already implied by
    the original (a paraphrase of the same fact, not a new fact).
C4. Semantic consistency (语义一致): preserve the original stance, the target of
    any criticism or support, the asserted facts, and the emotional intensity.
    Preserve sarcasm, metaphor and hedging exactly as they function in the
    original -- do not flatten them into neutral statements, and do not
    strengthen a hedged claim into a certain one.

[PROHIBITIONS]
- Do NOT add facts, numbers, names, dates or entities that are absent from the input.
- Do NOT resolve, verify, debunk, moralise about, or comment on the content.
- Do NOT translate, and do NOT change the language of the input.
- Do NOT output explanations, markdown fences, prefixes or suffixes.

[OUTPUT]
Return the rewritten JSON object only.
